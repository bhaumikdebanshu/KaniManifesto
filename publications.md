A proposed year-round publishing imprint with its own editorial identity, serving work across the organisation. “Kani Publications” is a working name.

Possible outputs:

- Research papers, essays, field notes and working publications.

- Residency journals, artists’ books and experimental editions.

- Exhibition catalogues and festival readers.

- Craft documentation, oral histories and community-authored publications.

- Learning resources, toolkits and field guides.

- Zines, digital editions, audio publications and interactive formats.

- Commissioned or partner-supported publications with an agreed editorial brief.

**Editor / Publishing Lead — retained or project-based initially**

Shapes the editorial programme, works with authors, develops manuscripts, coordinates review and checks sources, credit and permissions. Works with the Director on the publishing plan and with relevant programme owners on content.

Support comes from:

- Programme coordination for schedules, contributors, budgets and delivery.

- Design & Communications for publication design, promotion and launches.

- Commissioned copy editors, reviewers, translators, illustrators and other specialists.

- Fundraising support for suitable publishing partnerships and grants.

- Accounts and designated sales/distribution support for production costs, stock and receipts.

Every title has a named editorial owner, intended readership, format, budget and distribution plan. Decide whether each publication will be free, sold, commissioned or supported by a partner. Publishing income does not remove its research or cultural purpose.

Start with a small, funded editorial programme. The number and type of Year 1 titles remain to be agreed.
