## Purpose

A proposed year-round publishing imprint serving work across all three pillars. “Kani Publications” is a working name.

## Structure

```kani-chart
- Kani Publications | Agreed editorial plan and budget
  - Editor / Publishing Lead | Editorial direction and quality
    - Authors & contributors | Develop work with the editor
    - Editorial specialists | Review, editing and translation as needed
```

The Editor owns editorial quality within the agreed brief and budget. Programme coordination, Design & Communications, outreach, Partnerships, Accounts and distribution support are shared services rather than additional reporting lines.

```kani-details
| Role | Responsibility | Arrangement |
|---|---|---|
| Kani Publications | Sets priorities, resources and appointments through the annual programme and budget. | Programme responsibility within How Kani Works. |
| Editor / Publishing Lead | Develops the editorial programme, manuscripts, review, sources, credit and permissions. | Retained or appointed per title or series. |
| Authors & contributors | Develop original work and agree source, credit and permission arrangements. | Commissioned or agreed per publication. |
| Editorial specialists | Provide subject review, language editing and translation. | Commissioned according to need. |
```

## Possible formats

- Research papers, essays, field notes and residency journals.
- Artists’ books, experimental editions, catalogues and festival readers.
- Craft documentation, oral histories and community-authored work.
- Learning resources, zines, audio publications and interactive formats.

## Working arrangements

Start with a small funded programme. Every title has a named editor, readership, format, budget, permissions and distribution plan. Programme leads contribute subject knowledge; shared services are allocated explicitly.

Budget for editing, design, review, translation, production, outreach and distribution. Decide whether each title is free, sold, commissioned or partner-supported before production begins.
