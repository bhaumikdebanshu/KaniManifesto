**Current proposal—for discussion.** The following is a proposed structure, not a list of confirmed appointments.

The working team maintains continuity across Kani’s programmes and keeps the place functioning throughout the year.

## Starting team

| Role | Main responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Programme Coordinator** | Develops the year-round programme, connects initiatives, supports curators and facilitators, maintains programme relationships and carries learning between editions. |
| **Operations Coordinator** | Maintains the shared calendar, resource plan, venue readiness, working processes and coordination with accounts, hospitality and technical support. |
| **Design & Communications** | Maintains visual communication, programme announcements, audience information, registration communication and documentation coordination. |

These three roles work alongside the existing Accounts and Hospitality teams and draw on the [Advisory Circle](#structure-advisory) where specialist guidance or relationships are useful.

Two further responsibilities require explicit capacity:

| Responsibility | Starting arrangement |
|---|---|
| **Partnerships & Fundraising** | A named part-time or retained person develops institutional relationships, funding opportunities, proposals and reporting. |
| **Community & Care** | Allocated paid time within a suitable role, or part-time support, for local relationships, participation, feedback and practical team care. |

Combining responsibilities is possible where skills and time allow. It must not become an assumption that additional work happens without support or compensation.

## How the team works together

There is no separate Working Coordination Group. Coordination is part of the working team’s normal work.

A fortnightly meeting reviews:

- Upcoming programmes and commitments.
- Staff capacity and support needs.
- Space, equipment and scheduling conflicts.
- Partnership and funding developments.
- Decisions requiring council involvement.

Meetings become more frequent during busy periods. An agreed person maintains the action list; this does not make them the head of the team.

Each role has a short written mandate stating its responsibilities, decision authority, resources and review arrangements. Each person also has one named support and review contact, so feedback and instructions do not come from several directions.

Continuing coordination-role reviews are overseen by the council with team input. Members do not participate in decisions about their own appointment, pay or performance.

## How the team grows

Additional appointments follow demonstrated need and funding. They may include technical support, community engagement, commercial bookings or dedicated programme management.

Before adding a permanent role, consider whether the work is continuing, seasonal or specific to one programme. Festival peaks should carry their own staffing budget.
