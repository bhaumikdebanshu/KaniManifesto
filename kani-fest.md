An interdisciplinary festival with a fresh curatorial proposition for each edition. The previous format was a 15-day community festival; future scope and duration are agreed against resources.

**Festival Curator — rotating appointment**

Develops the artistic proposition and overall programme. Has final artistic say within the agreed brief and budget. Appointed for the development, delivery and close-out cycle, with preparation beginning sufficiently early—typically the 6–9 month cycle discussed.

Track appointments under the Festival Curator:

- Arts & New Media Curator.

- Music Curator.

- Craft & Material Practices Curator.

- Film & Moving Image Curator.

- Assistant curators or programme coordinators according to track workload.

Poetry, theatre, talks and interdisciplinary work are allocated explicitly within the curatorial brief. Collaborations may share budgets across tracks, with one named owner for each project.

**Delivery and continuity:**

- The Director agrees scope, budget, staffing and available infrastructure with the Festival Curator.

- In Year 1, the permanent coordinator works with a project-funded Festival Producer and temporary delivery team.

- At scale, the Creative Experiences Programme Manager provides continuity across editions, supported by Production and AV.

- The Festival Producer coordinates delivery; curators own artistic content. Neither role automatically assumes the other’s responsibilities.

- Each edition includes documentation, evaluation and handover.
