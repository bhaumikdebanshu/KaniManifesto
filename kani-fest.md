## Purpose

An interdisciplinary festival with a fresh curatorial proposition for each edition.

The Festival Curator owns artistic direction; the Festival Producer owns delivery. Neither automatically becomes a permanent post or council seat. The Programme Coordinator carries continuity between editions, while the Operations Coordinator connects the edition to shared resources.

## Programming flow

```kani-flow
- Curators develop proposals | Content, contributors and requirements
- Creative and resource review | Festival Curator + Festival Producer
- Confirmed schedule | Festival Schedule Coordinator
```

```kani-details
| Role / step | Responsibilities and purpose | Working relationship |
|---|---|---|
| Curators develop proposals | Each proposal states its purpose, participants, space, duration, budget, equipment and support needs. | The Festival Curator brings the tracks into one edition. |
| Creative and resource review | The Curator and Producer test artistic coherence and deliverability before commitments are made. | Unresolved scope or resource questions use the route in [Decisions & Programme Authority](#structure-decisions). |
| Confirmed schedule | One timetable records sessions, owners, setup and resource movements. | Changes are checked with affected leads and communicated once. |
```

## Volunteer pathway

```kani-flow
- Event volunteer | Defined tasks and supported shifts
- Returning volunteer / team captain | Bounded responsibility and training
- Paid associate / fellowship | Funded role with a clear brief
```

```kani-details
| Role / step | Responsibilities and purpose | Working relationship |
|---|---|---|
| Event volunteer | Joins with induction, an agreed shift, breaks and a named lead. | An entry point; continuing participation is optional. |
| Returning volunteer / team captain | Takes a bounded peer-support or coordination responsibility with training. | Sustained year-round work is scoped and paid. |
| Paid associate / fellowship | Holds a defined brief, supervision, learning outcomes, time and compensation. | Prior volunteering is neither required nor a guarantee of selection. |
```

## Curatorial fellowships and working rhythm

The four tracks are a working framework. They may be combined, and poetry, theatre, talks and cross-track work must be placed explicitly. Proposed fellowships cover research, local relationships, programme development, delivery, reflection and handover; fees, time, mentoring and production support are agreed before recruitment.

Preparation begins early enough for the fellowship cycle. Each edition budgets for production, outreach, documentation, evaluation and handover. See [People & Roles](#structure-people), [Operations & Resources](#structure-operations) and [Open decisions](#open-decisions).
