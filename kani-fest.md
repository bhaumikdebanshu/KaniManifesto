## Purpose

An interdisciplinary festival with a fresh curatorial proposition for each edition. The previous format was a 15-day community festival; future scope and duration are agreed against resources.

## Structure chart

```kani-chart
- Director of Creative Practice | Agrees scope, resources and appointments
  - Festival Curator | Artistic direction for the edition
    - Arts & New Media Curator | Track programme
    - Sound & Music Curator | Track programme
    - Craft & Material Practices Curator | Track programme
    - Film & Moving Image Curator | Track programme
    - Assistant curators | Assigned to their respective track leads
  - Festival Producer | Delivery plan and production coordination
    - Edition delivery team | Appointed crew and programme support
```

**Chart key:** Solid connections show project accountability and creative or delivery direction. Track assistants work through their assigned curator. The curator and producer collaborate; neither automatically takes on the other’s responsibilities. Permanent staff retain the reporting lines in [Full Structure](#full-structure).

> **Shared support — collaboration, not extra reporting lines:** Programme & Operations Coordinator in Year 1; Creative Experiences Programme Manager at scale; Operations / Production and AV; outreach; Design & Communications; Fundraising & Partnerships; Accounts; Hospitality. Their time and responsibilities are agreed in the edition plan.

## Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities and decisions | Working arrangement |
|---|---|---|
| Director of Creative Practice | Agrees the brief, budget, staffing and infrastructure; appoints leads and resolves resource conflicts. Financial commitments follow entity authority. | Continuing role. |
| Festival Curator | Develops the artistic proposition and programme; directs track curators; has final artistic say within the agreed brief and budget. | Rotating appointment for development, delivery and close-out. |
| Arts & New Media Curator | Develops the arts and new-media track, selects and supports practitioners, and coordinates requirements with the producer. | Edition appointment reporting artistically to the Festival Curator. |
| Sound & Music Curator | Develops music and sound programming, practitioner relationships and cross-disciplinary collaborations. | Edition appointment reporting artistically to the Festival Curator. |
| Craft & Material Practices Curator | Develops craft, material and maker-led activities with practitioners and community partners. | Edition appointment reporting artistically to the Festival Curator. |
| Film & Moving Image Curator | Develops screenings and moving-image work, contributor conversations and presentation requirements. | Edition appointment reporting artistically to the Festival Curator. |
| Assistant curators / programme coordinators | Support research, artist liaison, schedules and track delivery. Each has an assigned lead and clear scope. | Appointed against track workload; artistic questions go through the relevant curator, delivery arrangements through the producer. |
| Festival Producer | Coordinates the master delivery schedule, production budget tracking, vendors, crew and on-site delivery. Escalates feasibility and resource issues. | Project-funded appointment; reports on delivery to the Director and works alongside the Festival Curator. |
| Edition delivery team | Executes assigned production, coordination and visitor-facing tasks against the approved delivery plan. | Temporary crew and allocated staff; project tasks coordinated by the producer. |
| Programme continuity | Maintains calendar coordination, participant handovers, records and learning across editions. | Permanent coordinator in Year 1; Creative Experiences Programme Manager at scale. |
| Operations / Production and AV | Checks feasibility and venue readiness; coordinates installation, sound, projection and technical operation with the producer. | Allocated staff and appointed technical specialists. |
| Community engagement and outreach | Builds local relationships early, supports participation and access, and follows up after the edition. | Year 1 coordinator with Director support; dedicated outreach role when justified; additional edition support if needed. |
| Design & Communications | Leads programme announcements, audience communication, visual material, signage and documentation coordination. | Shared team with edition-specific support as required. |
| Fundraising & Partnerships | Develops funder and sponsor relationships and coordinates agreed reporting with programme and accounts inputs. | Retained support initially; dedicated manager as justified. |
| Accounts and Hospitality | Accounts supports financial records and payments; hospitality coordinates guest stays, food and visitor services. | Existing shared teams. |

## Working arrangements

Begin preparation sufficiently early—typically the 6–9 month cycle discussed. The Director agrees scope, budget, staffing and infrastructure with the Festival Curator and producer. Appoint and combine track roles according to the programme and resources; the chart does not require every role to be a separate hire.

Poetry, theatre, talks and interdisciplinary work are allocated explicitly within the curatorial brief. Collaborations may share budgets across tracks, with one named owner for each project.

In Year 1, the permanent coordinator works with a project-funded Festival Producer and temporary delivery team. At scale, the Creative Experiences Programme Manager provides continuity across editions, supported by Production and AV. Project appointments do not create extra permanent departments.

Each edition budgets for outreach, documentation, evaluation and handover, including follow-up with participants and partners. See [Outreach & Community Engagement](#outreach).
