## Simple versioning

| Version | When to use it |
|---|---|
| v0.1 · Alpha | This first presentation edition. |
| v0.2 · Revised draft | Current revision, following review of the Kani Fest model and first-edition experience. |
| v0.3 · Revised draft | A further meaningful round of discussion and changes. Not yet published. |
| v1.0 · Agreed baseline | When the group agrees a structure to begin working with. It can still evolve. |

Do not change the version for every typo. Increment it when the thinking, responsibilities or structure meaningfully changes. Record what changed, why and what remains unresolved; preserve the previous edition before publishing the next.

No presentation feedback has been recorded in this edition. For each item, record the suggestion, reason, affected pages, decision status, owner and next step.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Suggested | Raised for consideration; not an agreed change. |
| Agreed | Accepted for the next revision, with an owner and affected pages identified. |
| Deferred | Left open, with the reason or information needed to revisit it. |
