## Council structure

A proposed 14-seat council brings together advisors, serving curators, community and youth representatives. The Director attends as operational lead.

Heart and grace underpin how the council listens, collaborates and works with people.

Four perspectives come together in one council. The groups describe contributions, not separate departments or ranks. Members can contribute beyond their category.

| Group | Seats | Proposed roles and contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Practice & Systems Advisors | 5 | One each for Arts, Craft & Cultural Practice; Research, Learning & Publishing; Design, Systems & Technology; Business & Enterprise; Fundraising & Partnerships. Build capability, mentor the team and develop practitioner, institutional, industry and funding relationships. |
| Programme members — ex officio | 5 | Festival Curator; Arts & New Media Curator; Sound & Music Curator; Film & Moving Image Curator; Craft & Material Practices Curator. Bring current practice, practitioner relationships and programme experience. |
| Community representatives | 2 | Selected through a process developed with local residents, practitioners and participants. Bring priorities, access needs and lived experience; gather views and report back. |
| Youth representatives | 2 | Young people selected through an accessible youth-led nomination or selection process. Help shape opportunities, learning and Kani’s future direction. |
| **Total council seats** | **14** | The Director attends as operational lead, additional to these seats. Other working-team members join relevant items. |

Start with one advisor per area. Invite further specialists for particular enquiries rather than automatically adding standing seats. Community and youth seats are distinct places; one person does not fill two seats.

## Advisors and curators

An advisor supports the wider organisation across programmes and years. A curator develops a particular programme within an agreed brief. For example, the Arts, Craft & Cultural Practice Advisor helps build mentoring and institutional relationships; the Arts & New Media Curator develops the festival track.

Ex officio means membership by virtue of holding a role. Each serving curator joins through their appointment and leaves that seat when the appointment ends. These seats are additional to the advisors. An unfilled curatorial role leaves a vacant seat; no appointment is made solely to fill the council.

Advisors and community/youth representatives serve one-year terms, reviewed annually and renewable. Each has an agreed remit, time commitment and participation or compensation arrangement. Curatorial appointments include time for council work and handover.

Members step out of discussions concerning their own appointment, fees or performance. Interests in commissions, selections or partnerships are declared and handled through an agreed conflict process.

## How the council works

The council helps shape annual priorities, participation and programme learning, and advises on partnerships and long-term development. The Director and programme leads handle everyday delivery within agreed briefs and budgets. Financial and contractual commitments remain with authorised entity representatives.

One Convenor is appointed annually from the council. The full council meets quarterly, with temporary working groups for specific questions. Advisors agree practical contributions such as mentoring, introductions or helping develop organisational systems.

Community and youth representatives help set agendas, bring views from open community gatherings and report back on responses. Participation includes appropriate honoraria, travel and access support. Selection arrangements and decision procedures remain to be agreed.

Review the council structure after one year.
