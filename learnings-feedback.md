## A place to listen and learn

**Status: Feedback collection is ongoing.** This section holds programme experience, questions and suggestions separately from the manifesto. The structure below is provisional and can develop as feedback comes in.

## Kani Fest · Edition 1

The first edition ran for 15 days, with parallel workshops, talks and musical performances. Around 20 volunteers supported different tasks.

The notes below come from the organiser’s account shared during development of this document. They are initial observations, not a completed evaluation or a consensus from participants. Further feedback may add to or change them.

| Area | Initial observation | Questions to explore |
|---|---|---|
| Programming | Programming and parallel workshops and talks worked well. | What felt valuable to participants and contributors? Where did sessions overlap productively or compete? |
| Making and craft | Hands-on and craft workshops attracted strong interest. | Which formats, timings and facilitation worked best? What support or materials were missing? |
| Sound and music | Local musicians drew the largest crowds. | What made these gatherings accessible and engaging? How did artists experience them? |
| Timing | Around seven mornings were quiet across the 15-day edition. | Was this connected to timing, communication, audience availability or programme format? |
| Infrastructure | Shared equipment moved between spaces, and infrastructure was understaffed. | Where did clashes or delays occur? What crew, equipment and changeover time would have helped? |
| Production | There was no Festival Producer, and production capacity was weak. | Which responsibilities lacked an owner or adequate time? What needs dedicated coordination? |
| Volunteers | Volunteers supported varied tasks; some are interested in greater responsibility across the year. | What felt supported or difficult? Who wants to stay involved, and in what capacity? |

### Perspectives still to gather

Invite feedback from participants, local communities, artists, workshop facilitators, volunteers, curators, production and technical crew, hospitality, partners and the organising team. Ask what worked, what was difficult and what they would change.

## A simple feedback structure

Use one record for each distinct observation or suggestion. Keep the original feedback separate from our interpretation and any decision.

| Field | What to record |
|---|---|
| Programme / edition / date | Where and when the experience took place. |
| Perspective and source | Participant, contributor, volunteer, team or partner; how the feedback was collected. |
| Observation or feedback | What the person experienced, in their own words where appropriate. |
| Theme | Programme, access, communication, timing, infrastructure, volunteer experience, hospitality, Heart & Wellness or another relevant theme. |
| Question or suggestion | What needs understanding, testing or changing. |
| Status | Collected, exploring, agreed for action or deferred. |
| Response and follow-up | What was decided, why, who will follow up and when it will be reviewed. |

### Working statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Collected | Recorded; not yet reviewed with the relevant people. |
| Exploring | More perspectives, detail or a small test are needed. |
| Agreed for action | A response has been agreed, with an owner and review point. |
| Deferred | Kept for later, with the reason recorded. |
