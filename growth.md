Growth follows recurring work and funding rather than a fixed hiring sequence.

| Trigger | Staffing response |
|---|---|
| Credible funding opportunities require sustained follow-up. | Make Fundraising & Partnerships Manager permanent. |
| Residencies, public programmes and commercial hires regularly overlap. | Separate programme management from operations/production coordination. |
| Technical activity becomes frequent and predictable. | Bring in a permanent AV & Technical Operator. |
| Commercial enquiries and bookings become a substantial recurring business. | Add a Commercial & Bookings Executive under Fundraising & Partnerships. |
| Research and public programmes each sustain a funded calendar. | Create dedicated managers for Research, Residencies & Learning and Creative Experiences. |
| Publication work becomes a sustained, funded annual programme. | Expand the editor’s retained time; consider a continuing publishing role only when justified. |
| Festival or marketplace peaks exceed normal capacity. | Add temporary producers, coordinators and crew through the relevant programme budget. |

Before each permanent addition, confirm recurring need, a clear reporting line and visibility of funding for the role’s full cost over the following 12 months. This is a proposed planning rule, not a commitment to hire.
