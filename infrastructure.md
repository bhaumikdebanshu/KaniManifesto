This layer shows what every programme can draw on. It does not create additional posts or duplicate the permanent team.

| Shared capability | Resources and services | Responsible roles |
|---|---|---|

| Documentation & knowledge systems | Photography, recordings, archives, contributor information, consent and permissions records, publication files and programme learning. | Named programme owner, supported by communications, editors and commissioned documentation staff. |
| Hospitality | Guest arrangements, accommodation, catering and visitor support. | Existing Hospitality Team. |
| Heart & Wellness | Year-round welcome, check-ins, shared activities, morale and practical care. | Named coordinator with allocated time, accountable to the Director; all leads share responsibility for their teams. |

A shared planning meeting resolves competing demands on staff, spaces and equipment. Every request includes an owner, brief, deadline and budget.

## Shared resources during Kani Fest

The Festival Producer coordinates delivery with a named Technical & Infrastructure Lead. Maintain one equipment allocation and movement plan covering venues, operators, crew, setup and changeovers. Venue/session leads confirm readiness; programme changes go through the Programme Coordinator. Shared staff time and specialist crew are budgeted explicitly. Volunteers support assigned tasks under supervision. See [Kani Fest](#kani-fest).

## Heart & Wellness

A shared, year-round practice that helps people feel welcome, connected and supported at Kani. It brings together team care, morale and time spent together beyond programme delivery, for staff, residents, fellows, collaborators and volunteers.

A named coordinator holds the calendar and follow-up, with allocated time and a modest activity budget agreed with the Director. Programme leads, Hospitality and the Volunteer Coordinator contribute where relevant. Every lead remains responsible for respectful working and realistic workloads. Agree an alternative contact when a concern involves the usual lead.

### Activities throughout the year

This is a proposed rhythm to shape with the people taking part. Participation is optional, and timings and access needs should be considered together.

| Rhythm | Activities | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| On arrival and throughout a placement | A personal welcome, orientation, introductions and a peer contact. | Help new people feel at home and know where to ask for support. |
| Regularly during the working month | Short team check-ins, informal tea conversations and workload reviews. | Make space to listen, notice pressures early and agree practical support. |
| Monthly | A shared meal or potluck, a listening circle or an informal creative session. | Build relationships and exchange beyond assigned tasks. |
| Seasonally | Walks, outdoor time, playful making and skill-sharing sessions. | Create space for rest, enjoyment and connection to place. |
| Around busy programmes | Agreed breaks, food, rest spaces, support contacts and a team debrief. | Make care practical during periods of concentrated work. |
| At intervals across the year | Appreciation gatherings, reflection and a review of what people would like to continue or change. | Recognise contributions and let the programme evolve with the team. |

Keep the format light and responsive. Listening should lead to practical follow-through where needed; shared activities support, rather than substitute for, fair working arrangements.
