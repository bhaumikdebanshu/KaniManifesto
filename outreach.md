## Purpose

Outreach builds continuing relationships across all three pillars. Communications tells people what is happening; outreach helps people enter, shape and remain connected to the work.

## Four commitments

1. **Name an owner.** Every programme identifies who builds relationships and who follows up afterwards.
2. **Provide time and access support.** Outreach begins during programme development and budgets for visits, language, travel or other participation needs where relevant.
3. **Keep a shared relationship record.** Record interests, commitments and follow-ups with consent for continued contact. Personal contact details stay in an appropriate internal system.
4. **Close the feedback loop.** Record what was heard, what changed and who will respond.

## Who carries the work

The person holding **Community & Care** maintains local continuity. Programme leads remain responsible for commitments made through their work. Design & Communications supports audience information. The Partnerships lead maintains institutional and funding relationships.

Busy programmes budget for additional outreach capacity. Volunteer coordination remains a distinct delivery responsibility within Kanifest and other large events.

| Platform | Outreach focus |
|---|---|
| **Kanifest** | Local participation, invitations, access and relationships beyond the edition. |
| **Kani Publications** | Contributor relationships, credit, permissions and routes to intended readers. |
| **Curated Marketplaces** | Sustained artisan and maker relationships, accessible applications and participant feedback. |
