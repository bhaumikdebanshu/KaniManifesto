## Purpose

Outreach is a continuing responsibility across all three pillars. It builds relationships, helps Kani understand what people need, and creates ways for people to participate in shaping its work. Communications helps people hear about a programme; outreach helps them find a way into it and influence it.

## Relationships and responsibilities

| Area | What we do | Who leads |
|---|---|---|
| Local relationships | Meet neighbourhood groups, craftspeople, educators and cultural practitioners; listen to their interests and develop activities together. | Programme team; Programme & Operations Coordinator in Year 1. |
| Participation and access | Reach potential residents and participants; explain opportunities; identify barriers such as language, cost, timing or accessibility. | Named programme owner, supported by communications and outreach coordination. |
| Audience development | Build relationships with groups likely to attend, invite them personally and encourage repeat participation beyond one event. | Design & Communications, alongside programme owners. |
| Institutional connections | Develop exchanges and joint programmes with schools, colleges, cultural organisations and research institutions. | Director and relevant programme owner, with the partnerships lead where needed. |
| Funding and commercial relationships | Approach funders, sponsors, offsite clients and venue users with relevant proposals. | Fundraising & Partnerships / Commercial & Bookings. |

## Four working commitments

1. **A named owner.** Every programme identifies who handles outreach and maintains the relationships afterwards. Programme owners remain responsible for commitments made to participants.
2. **Time and budget.** Outreach begins while a programme is being developed. Budgets allow for visits, translation, travel and participation support where needed.
3. **A shared relationship record.** Record conversations, interests, commitments and follow-ups, with consent for ongoing contact. Contact details are held in an appropriate internal record, not this public document.
4. **A feedback loop.** Ask what worked, who was missing and what should change. Review repeat participation, collaborations and participant experience alongside attendance and communication reach.

## Year 1 and growth

The **Director of Creative Practice** is accountable for the overall approach and supports key relationships. The **Programme & Operations Coordinator** coordinates local relationships, participation support and follow-up. The **Design & Communications Associate** leads audience communication with programme inputs. The retained fundraising lead handles funding relationships and relevant institutional partnerships.

Allocate working time for these responsibilities. A busy festival, residency or marketplace must budget for additional outreach support when the permanent team cannot carry it.

As recurring work and funding justify it, a **Community Engagement & Outreach Coordinator** reports to the Director and works across the pillars. This role maintains continuity between programmes; programme owners still own their briefs, budgets and commitments.

## Across the platforms

- **Kani Fest:** Co-develop participation with local groups, plan invitations and access, and maintain relationships after the edition. Work with the Volunteer Coordinator on continuing participation; that coordinator owns induction, rota and welfare, while assigned venue or task leads direct each shift. Sustained year-round responsibilities need a scoped, funded role.
- **Kani Publications:** Invite contributors and community knowledge holders, agree credit and permissions, and plan how publications reach their intended readers.
- **Curated Marketplaces:** Build sustained artisan and maker relationships, support applications and participation, and gather feedback after each edition. Curatorial selection remains with the Marketplace Curator.

Programme reviews record what was heard, what changed in response and who will follow up. See [Decision rights](#decisions) for the boundaries between outreach, creative selection and financial approval.

## Care within the team

Outreach builds continuing relationships with communities and participants. The shared Heart & Wellness responsibility supports the people doing the work across Kani through welcome, check-ins, morale and practical support. The two work together where community members become collaborators or volunteers; volunteer rota and shift responsibilities stay with the assigned festival leads.

The [Heart & Wellness](#heart-wellness) section will be developed together; its activity programme remains open.
