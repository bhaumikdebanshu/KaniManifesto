| Decision | Responsible role |
|---|---|
| Overall direction and annual priorities | Council collectively, informed by the Director and working team. |
| Year-round programme, staff allocation and operational commitments | Director of Creative Practice, within delegated authority. |
| Festival artistic selections | Festival Curator, within the agreed brief and budget. |
| Publication editorial development and quality | Editor / Publishing Lead, within the agreed publishing brief and budget. |
| Marketplace theme and participant selection | Marketplace Curator, within the agreed programme brief. |
| Project schedules, participant coordination and delivery follow-up | Named programme manager or coordinator. |
| Technical feasibility, venue readiness and production methods | Operations / Production Lead or the coordinator with appointed technical specialists. |
| Funding proposals and funder relationships | Fundraising lead/manager with Director, programme and accounts inputs. |
| Commercial proposals and bookings | Named commercial owner within approved pricing and booking rules. |
| Contracts and financial commitments | Authorised representatives of the relevant entity. |

When scope exceeds agreed resources, the relevant creative lead and Director revise the proposal or seek additional support. Council endorsement alone does not authorise spending.
