| Decision | Responsible role |
|---|---|
| Overall direction and annual priorities | Council collectively, informed by the Director and working team. |
| Year-round programme, staff allocation and operational commitments | Director of Creative Practice, within delegated authority. |
| Festival artistic selections | Festival Curator, informed by track curators/fellows, within the agreed brief and budget. |
| Festival programme and resource review | Festival Curator and Producer jointly review the programme; the Curator owns artistic choices and the Producer owns delivery planning. Escalate unresolved resource conflicts to the Director. |
| Festival confirmed timetable | Programme Coordinator maintains the schedule and change log after review by the relevant leads. |
| Festival equipment allocation and changeovers | Technical & Infrastructure Lead under the Producer, working with venue/session leads and shared technical staff. |
| Festival volunteer coordination | Volunteer Coordinator owns induction, rota and support; one assigned venue or task lead directs each shift. |
| Publication editorial development and quality | Editor / Publishing Lead, within the agreed publishing brief and budget. |
| Marketplace theme and participant selection | Marketplace Curator, within the agreed programme brief. |
| Outreach strategy and key institutional relationships | Director, informed by programme owners and community feedback. |
| Community relationships, participation support and follow-up | Programme & Operations Coordinator in Year 1; dedicated outreach coordinator when appointed, working with the named programme owner. |
| Audience communication and development | Design & Communications, alongside programme owners and outreach coordination. |
| Project schedules, participant coordination and delivery follow-up | Named programme manager or coordinator. |
| Technical feasibility, venue readiness and production methods | Operations / Production Lead or the coordinator with appointed technical specialists. |
| Funding proposals and funder relationships | Fundraising lead/manager with Director, programme and accounts inputs. |
| Commercial proposals and bookings | Named commercial owner within approved pricing and booking rules. |
| Team care, morale and working culture | Director accountable; named People, Care & Team Culture coordinator supports check-ins and practical care. Every lead remains responsible for their team’s working conditions. |
| Contracts and financial commitments | Authorised representatives of the relevant entity. |

When scope exceeds agreed resources, the relevant creative lead and Director revise the proposal or seek additional support. Council endorsement alone does not authorise spending.

Every project has one named owner and a clear budget, including when it connects pillars or platforms. For shared projects, agree who owns each deliverable and how budgets and handovers are coordinated.

The council’s proposed voting method gives one vote to each advisory area, regardless of whether it has one or two members. It remains subject to collective agreement; see [Council appointments and decision procedure](#council) and [Open Decisions](#open-decisions).
