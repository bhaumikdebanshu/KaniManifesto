Programme teams bring particular initiatives to life. They draw on Kani’s shared resources while holding responsibility for their own creative and delivery work.

Each programme begins with a short agreement covering:

- Purpose, participants and intended contribution.
- Named creative and delivery responsibilities.
- Budget, funding and spending authority.
- Staff time, spaces, equipment and technical support.
- Timeline, communication, care and access needs.
- Review, documentation and handover.

For a small initiative, one person may hold both creative and delivery responsibility. Larger programmes need distinct roles.

## Kanifest

Kanifest has two complementary leads:

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Festival Curator** | Shapes the edition’s artistic proposition, mentors track curators and makes artistic selections within the agreed brief and budget. |
| **Festival Producer** | Builds and manages the delivery plan, including scheduling, staffing, technical requirements, suppliers and on-site coordination. |

Track curators develop their programmes with practitioners and communities. Their proposals enter a joint creative and resource review before commitments are confirmed.

The Festival Curator works closely with Kani’s Programme Coordinator. The Festival Producer works closely with Kani’s Operations Coordinator.

The edition budgets for scheduling, technical delivery, artist liaison, venue support, volunteer coordination and documentation according to its scale. These responsibilities do not automatically become permanent posts.

To distinguish the roles, the festival’s timetable responsibility is called **Festival Schedule Coordinator**; Kani’s Programme Coordinator holds the year-round remit.

## Connection to the council

Festival leads join council discussions concerning the edition’s brief, allocation, major changes and review. They can submit proposals and concerns directly.

Programme appointments do not automatically create council seats. Invited leads contribute to the discussion; formal council decisions remain with its appointed members. Within an approved programme, authority remains with the programme team.

## Other initiatives

The same approach applies to residencies, publications, marketplaces and Heart programmes. Each has a named lead, clear resources and an appropriate level of support.

At the end of a programme, the team hands over relationships, records, financial information and learning to the relevant continuing team member.
