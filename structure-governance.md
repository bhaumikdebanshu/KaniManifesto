The Kani Council holds responsibility for the organisation’s shared direction and accountability. It connects people doing the work with community perspectives and external experience.

## Council membership

| Members | Seats | Contribution |
|---|---:|---|
| Working-team representatives | 2 | Experience of programmes, operations, capacity and everyday working conditions. |
| Local community representatives | 2 | Local knowledge, priorities, participation and access. One seat is held by a young person. |
| External practitioners | 3 | Complementary experience in creative practice and learning, organisational development, and partnerships or enterprise. |
| **Total** | **7** | Members share responsibility for Kani as a whole. |

These are council responsibilities, not seven additional staff posts. Each person occupies one seat.

There is no separate advisory council. External council members contribute advice, mentoring and relationships. Other specialists can be invited for particular questions without becoming standing members.

## What the council does

The council:

- Protects Kani’s purpose and ethos.
- Agrees annual priorities and overall budget allocations.
- Reviews programme outcomes, financial information and working conditions.
- Agrees major partnerships and commitments within the relevant entity arrangements.
- Establishes clear responsibilities and decision-making boundaries.
- Oversees a fair process for appointing and reviewing continuing coordination roles.

The council does not select every artist, approve every purchase or issue competing instructions to staff.

## Selection and terms

Working-team representatives are chosen by the working team. Community representatives are selected through an accessible process developed with local participants, with young people involved in choosing the youth representative.

External practitioners are selected through open nominations and agreed criteria. For the first council, the organising team facilitates a founding meeting involving staff and community participants, records how the seven members were selected and publishes the proposed membership.

Members serve an initial one-year term, renewable through review. Staggered handovers help maintain continuity. Participation time, honoraria and access support are agreed in advance.

Representatives gather views from the people they represent and report back on decisions.

## Convening

One member serves as **Council Convenor** for a year. The Convenor gathers agenda items, ensures information is circulated and follows up on agreed actions.

Meeting facilitation can rotate. The Convenor has no casting vote or unilateral power to override decisions. Every member can put a matter on the agenda.

The council meets quarterly, with additional meetings for decisions that cannot wait. Members receive a short programme, finance and capacity update between meetings.

## Founders and enabling entities

Founders participate through defined roles. Any authority retained through ownership, funding or an enabling entity is stated openly.

The responsibilities of SoUF, KiLab and M/s Kani Home—including contracts, payments and authorised signatures—must be documented alongside the council’s mandate. Teams should know which decisions they can make and which need additional authorisation.
