Kani maintains shared services so programmes can draw on dependable infrastructure and support.

| Function | Who carries the work | Main responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| Programme calendar | Programme and Operations Coordinators | Agree dates, capacity and connections between activities. Operations maintains the shared calendar. |
| Spaces and equipment | Operations Coordinator, with technical support | Bookings, readiness, maintenance, inventory, operators and changeovers. |
| Institutional partnerships and funding | Named Partnerships lead | Develop relationships, proposals and agreements; maintain commitments and coordinate reporting. |
| Commercial enquiries | Named booking owner | Enquiries, quotations, visits and follow-up, using agreed pricing and availability. |
| Accounts | Accounts team | Financial records, invoices, payments and accessible budget reports. |
| Communications | Design & Communications | Confirmed programme information, announcements, registration communication and audience access information. |
| Hospitality | Hospitality team | Accommodation, arrivals, food and guest support within agreed capacity. |
| Community relationships and care | Named Community & Care owner, with programme leads | Local participation, feedback, welcome, access and practical support. |

The booking owner may sit within an existing role initially. It must be clear who follows an enquiry through to confirmation.

## Institutional partnerships

Council members and other advisors can introduce potential partners. The named Partnerships lead maintains the relationship and coordinates follow-through.

Programme leads shape the collaboration’s content. Operations assesses delivery capacity. Accounts helps establish costs and reporting requirements. Commitments are made only by someone with the relevant authority.

Relationships, contacts and agreements are recorded so they remain accessible when a person leaves.

## Budgets and spending

The council agrees overall allocations, informed by available funding and team capacity. Each programme has an authorised budget holder.

Routine expenditure proceeds within agreed limits. Additional funding requests or transfers affecting another programme require consultation and the appropriate approval.

Accounts provides a simple view of available, committed and spent funds. Approval and payment checks should involve distinct people.

## Shared resources

One calendar and resource plan covers public programmes, festivals, residencies and commercial bookings. Availability includes setup, staffing, maintenance and recovery time.

Commercial activity must account for its full demands on the place. Agree protected time for public and community programmes when planning the calendar.

Shared staff support is allocated explicitly. A programme cannot assume that communications, hospitality or technical staff are available simply because they are part of Kani.
