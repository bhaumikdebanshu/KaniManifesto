All continuing roles sit within one permanent working team led by the **Director of Creative Practice**. This is a growth model; appointments follow recurring need and funding rather than being simultaneous Year 1 hires.

## Programme management

- **Programme Manager — Research, Residencies & Learning:** Owns residencies, fellowships, research, educational relationships and participant support. Shapes the research and learning pipeline, from developing opportunities and selecting participants to coordinating mentors, partners and outcomes. Ensures participants receive clear support and that learning is documented and shared.

- **Programme Manager — Creative Experiences:** Owns the public calendar and coordinates delivery for exhibitions, music, film, workshops, walks, marketplaces and Kani Fest. Works with the Programme Manager — Research, Residencies & Learning when projects produce public events, ensuring that each project has one named owner and clear delivery responsibilities.

Both report to the Director. Where a residency produces a public event, the two managers agree the handover and responsibilities for each part.

## Community engagement and outreach

**Community Engagement & Outreach Coordinator — reports to the Director**

Maintains local and community relationships across programmes, coordinates participation and access support, keeps the shared relationship record and follows up on feedback. Works with programme managers on co-development and with Design & Communications on audience development. Programme owners retain responsibility for creative briefs, budgets, selections and commitments.

In Year 1 these duties are allocated to the Programme & Operations Coordinator with Director support. Establish the dedicated role when recurring relationships and participation work exceed that capacity and funding can sustain it. See [Outreach & Community Engagement](#outreach).

## Fundraising, partnerships and commercial development

**Fundraising & Partnerships Manager — reports to the Director**

This operational role works alongside the Advisory & Programme Council’s **Fundraising & Resource Development** advisory role. The advisor provides strategic guidance and relationships; the Manager owns day-to-day fundraising delivery, partnerships and reporting.

Owns the funding strategy and pipeline, funder relationships, grants, CSR partnerships, sponsorships, donors and institutional collaborations. Oversees commercial development and coordinates funder reporting.

Reporting to this manager:

- **Commercial & Bookings Executive:** Enquiries, proposals, quotations, site visits, follow-up, confirmed bookings and client relationships.

- **Proposal & Grant Writer:** Applications, concept notes, CSR and sponsorship proposals, pitch material and narrative reports. Can remain external or retained.

Programme managers design and deliver funded activities and collect outcome evidence. Accounts supplies expenditure records and financial reporting. The Fundraising Manager coordinates the combined report and funder relationship.

## Operations and technical delivery

**Operations / Production Lead — reports to the Director**

Owns venue readiness, the master venue calendar, logistics, maintenance coordination, vendors and on-site delivery.

- **AV & Technical Operator — reports to the Operations Lead:** Operates sound, projection and screening systems, maintains equipment and coordinates with specialist vendors.

- External production partners provide additional capacity per project.

## Design and communications

**Design & Communications Lead — reports to the Director**

Owns visual communication, social media planning, audience outreach, announcements, newsletters, presentations and signage across all programmes.

- **Student Design Intern — reports to the Design Lead:** Supports production through a supervised rotating placement.

- Programme owners provide accurate information; the communications lead coordinates public-facing editing and approval.

- Editors own publication manuscripts and editorial quality. Publication design is planned with the design team.

- Photography, recording and editing receive allocated time or commissioned support.

## Existing support teams

- **Accounts Team:** Operationally coordinated under the Director; works with project owners and fundraising staff while retaining entity-specific approval procedures.

- **Hospitality Team:** Operationally coordinated under the Director; works with production, programme and bookings staff on delivery.

## Kani Fest project team

The Festival Curator and Festival Producer work alongside one another under the Director. Proposed 6–9 month paid curatorial fellows develop the tracks; the Producer coordinates programme scheduling, technical infrastructure, venue/session leads, artist liaison and volunteer coordination. These are edition appointments or allocated responsibilities, not automatic additions to the permanent team. The permanent coordinator in Year 1, or Creative Experiences Programme Manager at scale, holds continuity between editions. See the [festival working map and role explainers](#kani-fest).

## People, Care & Team Culture

A shared function with a named coordinator accountable to the Director, supporting staff, fellows, project teams and volunteers across Kani. It coordinates welcome, check-ins, recognition, listening and practical care. It can be allocated within an existing role initially; dedicated capacity follows workload and funding. All team leads retain responsibility for respectful working, realistic workloads and follow-through on concerns. See [Shared Infrastructure](#infrastructure).
