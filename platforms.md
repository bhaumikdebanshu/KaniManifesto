## Purpose

Develop recognisable programmes through which Kani’s ethos and pillars take shape. A platform can continue across years while contributors, themes and editions change.

An original initiative can begin as a small experiment and become a continuing platform if its value, audience, workload and funding justify it.

“Platforms & Original Initiatives” is the working title for this layer. Ownership and usage of particular works, brands and commissioned outputs are addressed through the relevant contributor agreements.

## Platform overview

| Platform | Relationship to the pillars | Creative lead |
|---|---|---|
| Kani Fest | Primarily Creative Experiences; draws on research, learning and partnerships. | Festival Curator with proposed 6–9 month paid track fellows; Festival Producer leads delivery alongside the Curator. |
| Kani Publications | Works across all three pillars; publishes research, creative practice and appropriate commissioned work. | Editor / Publishing Lead on retainer or project appointment initially. |
| Kani Curated Marketplaces | Primarily Creative Experiences; connects craft and material research with public exchange and maker livelihoods. | Marketplace Curator appointed for a series or edition. |
| Nurupa · in development | An initiative within Heart & Wellness, connecting reflective learning and sustained creative practice with community exchange. | Programme lead, mentors and facilitators to be confirmed against the programme brief and budget. |
| Future original initiatives | Assigned according to their purpose; may connect several pillars. | Named project lead appointed when the initiative is approved. |

The established platform pages include a structure chart and a responsibilities table; Nurupa’s developing approach is outlined in [Heart & Wellness](#heart-wellness). The charts distinguish project reporting from shared support; retained and project appointments are not additional permanent Year 1 hires. [Outreach & Community Engagement](#outreach) supports participation across all platforms. Shared [Heart & Wellness](#heart-wellness) supports team morale, listening and practical care across their working teams.

The Director oversees platform development and resource planning with council guidance. There is no separate permanent “IP creation” department in Year 1.

[Heart & Wellness](#heart-wellness) also holds programmes for growth, wellbeing and becoming, including Nurupa in development. Each initiative needs a defined brief, named lead and funded delivery capacity; detailed arrangements remain open.
