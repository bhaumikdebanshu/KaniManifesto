This page applies to organisational, advisory, creative and operational appointments. Selection of programme participants is covered under [Programme Selection](#programme-selection).

Appointments are where people receive responsibility and authority. The process should therefore be understandable, proportionate and protected from control by any one person.

## What principles guide appointments?

Appointments should be based on:

- A clear need and written role
- Relevant capability, experience and relationships
- Alignment with Kani’s ethos
- Fair compensation and realistic time expectations
- Appropriate representation and access
- Declared conflicts of interest
- Defined authority and accountability
- A term or review point
- A planned handover

Appointment to an advisory, programme or operational role does not automatically create a Council seat or authority beyond the written mandate.

## What appointment routes can Kani use?

| Route | When it may be appropriate |
|---|---|
| **Representative selection** | Council seats representing the Working Team, communities or young people |
| **Open application** | Continuing paid roles or opportunities where wider access and comparison are valuable |
| **Open nomination** | Council, advisory or specialist roles where relevant groups can put people forward |
| **Invitation** | A role requiring a particular practice, relationship, experience or form of knowledge |
| **Commission** | A defined creative, curatorial or specialist contribution |
| **Internal allocation** | A bounded responsibility assigned within an existing paid role |
| **Procurement** | Vendors or professional services selected against scope, cost and capability |

Not every role requires an open call. The chosen route and the reason for it should be stated before selection begins.

## Who appoints whom?

| Appointment | Decision route |
|---|---|
| Working Team representatives to the Council | Selected by the Working Team |
| Community representatives | Selected through an accessible process developed with relevant local participants |
| Youth representative | Selected through a process that meaningfully involves young people |
| Founding and enabling-entity representatives | Named through a transparent process that states the authority they carry |
| Independent public-interest or practitioner representatives | Selected through open nomination or expression of interest by a non-conflicted group including Council, Working Team and community representation |
| Council Convenor | Chosen by the Council from among its members for a limited term |
| Advisory Circle members | Nominated or invited for defined expertise and confirmed by the Council after appropriate consultation |
| Continuing Working Team roles | Selected by a non-conflicted appointment group established by the Council, with relevant team and specialist input |
| Programme leads and curators | Selected through the route named in the approved programme brief |
| Programme contributors and participants | Selected through the approved programme’s people-selection process |
| Temporary specialists and vendors | Appointed by the authorised programme or operational lead within the approved budget and procurement process |

An enabling entity may need to issue an employment contract or sign an agreement. That legal action should follow the agreed appointment decision rather than silently replace it.

## What happens before selection?

Every appointment begins with a short role brief stating:

- Why the role exists
- Responsibilities and expected contribution
- Decision authority and limits
- Reporting, support and review relationships
- Time commitment and working period
- Compensation, honorarium or fee
- Required experience or capability
- Selection route and decision-makers
- Conflicts that must be declared
- Term, review and possible renewal

The opportunity should be communicated in a form appropriate to the people Kani hopes to reach. Access support should be available where relevant.

## How is the decision made?

The appointment group:

1. Reviews the role brief and agreed criteria.
2. Declares personal, professional and financial conflicts.
3. Considers applications, nominations or invited candidates.
4. Seeks relevant perspectives without circulating private information unnecessarily.
5. Records the decision and its reasons.
6. Confirms compensation, authority, support and review arrangements.
7. Communicates the outcome respectfully.

A person does not participate in deciding their own appointment, pay, renewal or performance review.

For invited appointments, Kani records why the invitation is appropriate. For open processes, all candidates are assessed against the same stated criteria.

## What terms apply?

| Role | Proposed term |
|---|---|
| Council member | One year initially, renewable through review, with staggered handovers where possible |
| Council Convenor | One year, with no additional authority beyond the Convenor’s stated responsibilities |
| Advisory Circle member | One year, stating contribution, time, honorarium and review point |
| Continuing Working Team role | Continuing appointment with an initial review period and regular role review |
| Programme lead or curator | The period defined by the programme brief, including preparation, delivery and handover |
| Temporary specialist | The period and deliverables defined in the agreement |

Renewal is not automatic. It considers contribution, conduct, capacity, changing organisational needs and the value of bringing in new perspectives.

## How are appointments reviewed?

A review considers:

- Whether the role remains necessary
- Whether its mandate is still clear
- Contribution and follow-through
- Workload, support and compensation
- Relationships and working conduct
- Conflicts of interest
- Learning and development
- Whether the role should continue, change, conclude or be reopened

The person being reviewed receives the relevant feedback and an opportunity to respond.

## How can an appointment conclude early?

An appointment may conclude because:

- The agreed work has finished
- The role is no longer required or funded
- The person resigns
- Responsibilities are repeatedly not fulfilled
- A conflict makes the role untenable
- Serious conduct or misuse-of-authority concerns are upheld
- The appointment process or mandate is found to be invalid

Except where immediate protective action is necessary, the person receives notice of the concern, a fair opportunity to respond and a reasoned decision by non-conflicted people.

Interim arrangements are time-limited and do not become permanent appointments without the appropriate process.

## What is public and what remains private?

Kani may publish:

- The names and mandates of Council members and advisors
- Role titles and organisational responsibilities
- Appointment routes and terms
- Conflicts relevant to public decision-making

Applications, references, personal information, compensation details and sensitive review material remain appropriately private.

Every concluding appointment includes a handover of responsibilities, records, relationships and organisational property.
