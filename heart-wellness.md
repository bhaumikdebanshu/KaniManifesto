## Heart as our foundation

Whatever we do, we do with heart and grace. Heart is the ground beneath all three pillars: care for ourselves, one another and the places we inhabit. It shapes how we listen, create, learn and work together.

Heart also has a living programme: experiences that help people explore who they are, practise who they wish to become and grow towards their potential. Reflection, creativity, companionship and sustained practice connect inner development with how we live and contribute to the world.

## Two connected expressions

| Expression | What it makes possible |
|---|---|
| Everyday culture of care | Welcome, belonging, listening, team check-ins, rest, recognition and practical support across Kani. |
| Programmes for growth and becoming | Guided journeys, reflective residencies, creative practice, mentorship, mindful gatherings and community learning throughout the year. |

Heart remains a foundation shared by the three pillars. Its programmes connect with research, residencies and learning, and can lead to creative experiences and public exchange.

## Nurupa · an initiative in development

Nurupa is a proposed journey of remembering, practising and expressing our potential through play, reflection and creative practice. Its working white paper describes an offering of the **Qalb Center at Kani Home**, as part of **The Slow Thing Festival**. These programme identities and arrangements remain in development.

Participants imagine an aspirational self—an avatar—and explore its qualities through daily practice, supported by mentors and fellow participants. A craft or passion project gives that exploration a tangible form. The proposed winter-to-spring journey brings together on-site and online participation, with a spring sharing of what participants have practised and made.

| Programme element | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Reflection and self-enquiry | Make room to notice habits, aspirations and possibilities through journaling, quiet time and conversation. |
| Aspirational avatar and play | Explore ways of being through an imagined identity, cooperative games and shared discovery. |
| Creative practice | Develop a craft or passion project through regular, sustained attention. |
| Mentorship and companionship | Offer encouragement, listening and reflection within a supportive community. |
| Rhythm, rest and mindful attention | Create space to slow down and practise with care and at a personally appropriate pace. |
| Sharing and continuation | Share chosen work and learning, then continue, adapt, mentor or rest. |

Nurupa is one expression of Heart. Other programmes can take different forms while sharing the intention of helping people grow with care, agency and connection. The white paper is a working proposal, not a confirmed calendar or a promise of particular outcomes.

## Year-round possibilities

- Reflective residencies and seasonal journeys combining personal enquiry with creative practice.
- Regular circles for listening, journaling, storytelling and shared reflection.
- Mindfulness, movement and wellbeing sessions with appropriate facilitators.
- Mentored craft and passion projects, with optional opportunities to share work.
- Community gatherings that nurture belonging, mutual support and continued practice.

## How programmes are held

The Director oversees the programme direction. Each initiative has a named lead, defined facilitator and mentor responsibilities, allocated time and a budget. The shared Heart & Wellness coordinator connects this work with everyday care across Kani; programme delivery receives its own capacity.

Participation respects different beliefs, backgrounds and personal boundaries. People choose what they share and may opt out of activities. Recording, documentation and public sharing require clear consent; personal reflections are not automatically publication material. Facilitator preparation, participant support, privacy and any practice-specific safeguards must be agreed before launch.

Nurupa's calendar, cohort format, mentor appointments, access and pricing, online arrangements and support protocols remain open for collective development. See [Open decisions](#open-decisions). Learning from each programme belongs in [Learnings & Feedback](#learnings-feedback), using participant-approved or anonymised summaries.
