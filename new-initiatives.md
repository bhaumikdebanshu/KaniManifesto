Use a simple progression:

1. Identify an enquiry, need or opportunity connected to the ethos and pillars.

2. Define a small pilot with a named owner, audience, budget and outcome.

3. Deliver and document the pilot.

4. Review value, local relevance, participation, workload and financial viability.

5. Repeat or revise where justified.

6. Establish a continuing platform only when there is a clear reason and capacity to sustain it.
