## Our ethos

**Proposed wording for review:**

Kani Home is a place for creative practice, research, learning and public exchange. It brings artists, designers, architects, researchers, craftspeople, musicians, ecologists, climate practitioners, heritage specialists, technologists and communities into conversation, creating space for both sustained enquiry and shared experiences.

Its work is guided by:

- **Heart & grace:** Bring care, openness and respect to everything we do. Support people in exploring who they are and who they wish to become, through reflection, creative practice and community.

- **Community participation:** Develop programmes with local people, practitioners and knowledge holders.

- **Experimentation:** Support exploration, making, testing and work whose outcomes are not predetermined.

- **Ecological responsibility:** Engage with climate, water, materials and the relationships between people and their environments.

- **Exchange across disciplines:** Connect art, design, craft, music, research and technology.

- **Continuity of knowledge:** Document, publish and share work so that learning continues beyond an event or residency.

- **Sustainable operation:** Develop earned income and external support that sustain the place, its people and its programme.

## How we work together

Kani brings different disciplines and forms of knowledge into shared enquiry. An artisan, technologist, researcher and local community might shape a project together, each influencing its questions, methods and outcomes. The three pillars give this work a structure while allowing projects to move between learning, public exchange and commissioned experiences.

[Outreach & Community Engagement](#outreach) builds the continuing relationships that make this participation possible, from listening and co-developing ideas to supporting access and following up after a programme.

[Heart](#heart-wellness) is both our way of being together and a space for growth. Through programmes such as Nurupa, we bring reflection, play, creative practice and mentorship together, helping people explore their potential and how they contribute to the world around them.
