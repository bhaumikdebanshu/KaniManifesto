## How the organisation fits together

| Layer | What it explains |
|---|---|
| Ethos | The values that guide Kani’s choices. |
| Heart & Wellness | The shared foundation of care and grace, expressed through everyday culture and programmes for growth, wellbeing and becoming, including Nurupa in development. |
| Three pillars | The broad kinds of activity Kani undertakes. |
| Outreach & Community Engagement | How Kani builds relationships, listens, enables participation and sustains connections across its programmes. |
| Kani Council | The proposed shared direction, annual priorities and accountability; see The Structure. |
| Advisory Circle | Specialist knowledge, mentoring, critical review and relationships. |
| Permanent working team | The people responsible for continuity, development and delivery. |
| Shared infrastructure & capabilities | The spaces, equipment and services available to every programme. |
| Platforms & Original Initiatives | The recognisable programmes Kani develops, including Kani Fest, Kani Publications and curated marketplaces. |

Heart underpins every pillar. Platforms bring the pillars into practice, drawing on one working team and shared infrastructure. Outreach continues across all three pillars.

[The Structure](#structure) presents the current organisational proposal; [Earlier Structures](#structure-archive) preserves earlier models.

## How a project moves across the pillars

The ethos supports transdisciplinary enquiry: people with different practices and forms of knowledge shape the questions, methods and outcomes together. The pillars describe the different activities through which that enquiry can develop.

**Example:** An artisan, technologist and local knowledge holders develop an AR heritage prototype during a residency. It is shared through a public walk and documented in a publication. An institution may later commission a related learning experience. The enquiry connects research and learning, public exchange and commissioned work; publishing carries its knowledge across all three.
