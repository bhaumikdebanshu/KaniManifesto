| Layer | What it explains |
|---|---|
| Ethos | The values that guide Kani’s choices. |
| Three pillars | The broad kinds of activity Kani undertakes. |
| Advisory & Programme Council | The expertise, relationships and strategic guidance supporting the work. |
| Permanent working team | The people responsible for continuity, development and delivery. |
| Shared infrastructure & capabilities | The spaces, equipment and services available to every programme. |
| Platforms & Original Initiatives | The recognisable programmes Kani develops, including Kani Fest, Kani Publications and curated marketplaces. |

These layers describe different aspects of one organisation. Platforms draw on the three pillars and share people and infrastructure; they do not each need a separate permanent department.

The pillars are the broad framework for activities. The lists below are a programme menu, not a commitment to launch everything in Year 1.

## Connections between the pillars

A project can connect several pillars while retaining one named owner and a clear budget.

**Example:** An artisan and technologist develop an AR heritage prototype during a residency. It is shared through a public walk and documented in a publication. An institution may later commission a related learning experience.
