| Layer | What it explains |
|---|---|
| Ethos | The values that guide Kani’s choices. |

Heart underpins every pillar; its programmes connect learning, creative practice and community rather than forming a fourth pillar. See [Heart & Wellness](#heart-wellness).

Platforms bring the pillars into practice, drawing on a shared team and infrastructure. Outreach is a continuing function across the three pillars, with responsibilities assigned to the working team.

The pillar chapters describe possible activities. Year 1 priorities will be selected according to capacity, partnerships and funding.

[The Structure](#structure) presents the current proposal: a small council, a continuing working team and programme teams. Earlier council and staffing proposals are preserved in [Earlier Structures](#structure-archive). Existing detailed programme charts retain earlier reporting arrangements pending collective agreement and alignment.

## How a project moves across the pillars

The ethos supports transdisciplinary enquiry: people with different practices and forms of knowledge shape the questions, methods and outcomes together. The pillars describe the different activities through which that enquiry can develop.

**Example:** An artisan, technologist and local knowledge holders develop an AR heritage prototype during a residency. It is shared through a public walk and documented in a publication. An institution may later commission a related learning experience. The enquiry connects research and learning, public exchange and commissioned work; publishing carries its knowledge across all three.
